3. CROSS-SITE REQUEST FORGERY (CSRF)
* Malicious URL Crafted: http://192.168.1.67:8081/transfer?to=Attacker&amount=5000
* Concept: What is the primary defense against CSRF?
Answer: The primary defense against CSRF is anti-CSRF 
tokens — unique, secret values embedded in forms that the server validates on every state-changing request, ensuring the request originated from a
legitimate page rather than a forged one.
